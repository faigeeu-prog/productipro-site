How to create a profitable Skool community: the complete guide.

Setting up a Skool space takes an evening. Turning it into steady income takes a method. Here are the steps we follow for every community we launch.

1. Define the promise

A paid community sells a specific transformation, not access to content. “Join my community” is not enough. “Land your first three freelance clients in 60 days” gives people a clear reason to join, and a reason to stay.

The best starting point: the questions that keep coming up under your content. They tell you exactly what your audience wants to learn.

2. Pick the right price

Most paid communities charge between $29 and $99 per month. The more concrete and money-related the result, the higher the price can go. Avoid pricing too low: it attracts less committed members who leave quickly.

Factor in Skool’s fees: the Hobby plan takes 10% of every payment, the Pro plan 2.9% for $99 per month.

3. Structure the path

Organize the Classroom into short modules, from simple to advanced. Lock the advanced modules behind levels: members earn points by taking part and unlock what comes next. That’s Skool’s gamification, and one of the strongest retention levers.

  • A welcome module of ten minutes at most.
  • Three to five modules that lead to the promised result.
  • An advanced module reserved for higher levels.

4. Launch to your audience

No ads needed. Open a waitlist a week before, announce the opening date in stories and posts, then open registrations for a limited time. A first cohort of founding members creates urgency and gives the community energy from day one.

5. Engage to keep members

The main enemy of a paid community is churn. It happens when the feed goes quiet. Plan one challenge a week, one Q&A session a month, and personally follow up with members who haven’t logged in for ten days.

6. Open the door to your premium offer

This is the step most creators forget. Your most engaged members know you, trust you and have already made progress with you: they are your best prospects for $1,500 to $5,000 coaching. Add an application form inside the community and only talk to people who are ready.

That is exactly what our Skool agency does: the community becomes recurring revenue, and a steady source of premium clients.
