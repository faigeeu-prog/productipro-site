Community management agency

Community management agency: we run your online community, and make it profitable.

Productipro is a community management agency focused on private and paid communities, on whichever platform fits your audience: Skool, Circle or Discord. We build them, run them every day and turn them into income for creators, coaches and experts.

Community management vs. social media management

Social media management means posting and replying on public networks: Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok. Community management, the way we do it, happens in a private space where your members meet, learn and progress together.

The difference matters: on social media, you rent an algorithm’s attention. In a private community, you build a place you own, with members who pay to be part of it.

What our community management includes

  • Building the space: structure, categories, rules, onboarding and course path.
  • Daily engagement: posts, replies to members, weekly challenges, Q&A sessions.
  • Moderation: a healthy, spam-free space where everyone feels free to take part.
  • Retention: tracking inactive members and churn, personal follow-ups, levels and rewards.
  • Monetization: a monthly membership, then an upgrade path to your premium offer.
  • Monthly report: members, churn, revenue, premium sales.

A community management agency paid on results

Most agencies charge a fixed monthly retainer, whether the community earns anything or not. We work differently: a one-time setup fee, then a share of what the community generates (50% of memberships, 20% of premium sales that come from the community). If the community earns nothing, neither do we. Details are on the home page.

Which platforms

The platform is a tool, not the strategy. We choose it with you based on where your audience already is and how you want to sell: Skool for courses and gamification, Circle for a branded space, Discord for gaming and tech audiences, Whop for digital products, or a simple WhatsApp or Telegram group for a close-knit community. What makes a community profitable is the promise, the engagement and the path to your premium offer, wherever it lives.

Fully remote

Community management happens entirely online. We work with creators in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and beyond.
