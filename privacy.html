Privacy policy

This policy explains what data Productipro collects, why, and what your rights are, in accordance with the GDPR.

Data controller

Productipro. Contact: contact@productipro.fr.

Data we collect

  • When you contact us: name, contact details, links to your social accounts and any information you choose to share, via WhatsApp or email.
  • Technical data strictly necessary for the website to work securely.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Reviewing your request: pre-contractual steps.
  • Website security: legitimate interest.
  • Accounting obligations: legal obligation.

Communities we run for clients

When we run a community for a creator, the creator remains the data controller for their members’ data. Productipro acts on their behalf under a written agreement.

Retention

  • Requests that do not lead to a project: 3 years from the last contact.
  • Clients: for the duration of the relationship, then archived as required by law.

Fonts and cookies

Fonts are loaded from Fontshare. This website uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability: write to contact@productipro.fr. You may also contact the French data protection authority (CNIL).
