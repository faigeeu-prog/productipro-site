Skool agency

Skool agency: we build and run your paid community.

Productipro is a done-for-you Skool agency. We build your community on Skool, announce it to your audience, run it every day, then guide your most engaged members to your high-ticket offer.

What a Skool agency does

Skool puts a course, a discussion feed, an events calendar and member payments in one place. The tool is simple. Everything around it is hard: structuring the content, making people want to join, keeping the space active every day and stopping members from leaving after two months.

A Skool agency does that work for you. At Productipro, it covers four steps:

  • Build: course path, levels, rewards and onboarding for new members.
  • Launch: the posts, stories and emails that open the community to your audience.
  • Engage: weekly challenges, Q&A sessions, moderation and churn tracking.
  • Convert: a path that prepares your most engaged members to join your premium coaching.

Why hire an agency instead of doing it yourself

Starting a Skool community takes an evening. Keeping it alive takes a daily presence: answering, following up, running challenges, spotting members who drift away. That invisible work is the difference between a community stuck at a few dozen members and one that becomes real income.

Most creators don’t have that time, and that’s normal: their job is to create content. An agency lets you keep your publishing rhythm while the community grows.

Skool agency vs. freelancer

Marketplaces are full of freelancers who set up a Skool space for a few hundred dollars. They deliver a clean space, then stop. An agency like Productipro stays after launch: engagement, retention and conversion to your premium offer are part of the job, and our pay depends on what the community earns.

What Skool costs, and what the agency costs

Skool has two plans in 2026: Hobby at $9 per month with a 10% transaction fee, and Pro at $99 per month with a 2.9% fee. For a paid community with more than a few dozen members, Pro is almost always the better deal.

Productipro works with a one-time setup fee, then a share of revenue: 50% of memberships and 20% of the premium sales that come from the community. If the test launch makes no sales, the setup fee is refunded. Full details are on the home page.

Our 4-week method

  • Week 1: audience review, community promise and pricing.
  • Week 2: Skool build, course path, levels and the upgrade path to your premium offer.
  • Week 3: waitlist and launch posts, written in your voice.
  • Week 4: seven-day test launch, then opening and daily engagement.

Who it’s for

Creators, coaches and experts with at least 20,000 engaged followers or a newsletter with 5,000 readers, a skill their audience wants to learn, and a premium offer (or the will to create one). The niches that work best: business and freelancing, video editing, content creation, e-commerce, real estate, fitness.
